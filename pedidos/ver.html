06
ENGINEERING LOGIC

Construction Engineering

Glute Enhancement Construction System

!CRITICAL

The shaping effect must be engineered through the knit structure.

The shaping effect must NOT be created through:

  • Scrunch seam
  • Gathered seams
  • Exaggerated pattern shaping
  • Excessive back rise

The visual shaping effect must be achieved through:

  • Knit Mapping
  • Engineered Knit
  • Targeted compression
  • Horizontal stretch distribution
  • Engineered knit zones
07
DEVELOPMENT NOTES

Development Notes

↳

Unlike other performance leggings, this style must not create glute enhancement by increasing the back rise.

Visual shaping must be achieved primarily through seamless knit engineering and the strategic placement of compression zones.

THIS CONSTRUCTION PRINCIPLE MUST BE MAINTAINED THROUGHOUT DEVELOPMENT.
